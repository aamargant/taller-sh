Custom Business Intelligence

How your business spawns new intelligences.

Standard SaaS platforms force your business into rigid, cookie-cutter templates. With taller.sh, you gain a foundational intelligence engine tailored exclusively to your operations.

As your company expands, you can spawn specialized sub-agents with dedicated permissions: an agent that verifies supplier invoices, an agent that schedules clinic consultations, or an agent that drafts inventory reorders.

Each sub-agent inherits your company's operational context, protocols, and vocabulary, while maintaining strict isolation so sensitive data stays compartmentalized.

Your core business knowledge (pricing rules, FAQs, SOPs, vendor catalogs) is embedded into private vector databases with semantic retrieval.

2. Autonomous Task Delegation

Parent agents analyze incoming requests and delegate sub-tasks to specialized worker agents, executing complex multi-step pipelines without human bottlenecks.

3. Continuous Skill Accumulation

Every time an engineer or staff member guides an agent through an edge case, that solution becomes a persistent skill saved to your private database.
