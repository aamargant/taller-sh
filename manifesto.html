ARTICLE I // INFRASTRUCTURE OWNERSHIP

Infrastructure Ownership vs. Cloud Tenancy

For the last fifteen years, businesses were told that owning nothing was progress: move every workflow, customer record, and internal communication onto rented third-party platforms, pay per seat forever, and hope pricing doesn't surge or service terms don't shift.

When software is engineered properly—on open standards, PostgreSQL, SQLite, Docker, and standard Linux—a €6/month VPS can run an entire business reliably for a decade. If you ever part ways with the engineers who wired it, the system stays running on your own servers without interruption. True software ownership means nobody can revoke your operational capabilities.

ARTICLE II // LOCAL AI MEMORY

Self-Improving Autonomous Agents

When you paste internal work into a corporate SaaS chatbot, you are training someone else's closed commercial moat.

When you run a dedicated autonomous agent on your own server, a closed learning loop operates directly on your storage disk: it synthesizes and saves procedural skills after solving complex operational tasks, indexes past sessions in local SQLite (state.db), runs unattended scheduled batch shifts, and allows you to swap foundational LLM backends without vendor lock-in. Over time, your server compounds institutional knowledge tailored to your exact business—and every byte of that wisdom belongs to you.

ARTICLE III // DATA SOVEREIGNTY BY PHYSICS

Security Enforced by Topology

Corporate privacy policies are subject to unilateral changes. One corporate acquisition, board pivot, or silent terms update turns yesterday's "private workspace" into tomorrow's training dataset or vulnerability headline.

Genuine security is architectural: strict TLS termination at your own gateway, container-isolated execution sandboxes, encrypted nightly snapshots to storage buckets you hold the master keys to, and customer databases that never leave your perimeter. Third parties cannot breach, train on, or subpoena data they never hold in the first place.

ARTICLE IV // SENIOR CREWS & ETHICS

High-Performance Teams & Social Value

We do not build autonomous systems to replace human connection. We build them so small, high-performance engineering teams can stand toe-to-toe with tech monopolies without burning out or sacrificing their values.

When software stops siphoning monthly tribute into distant corporate balances, capital stays local. It fairly compensates engineers, keeps independent businesses profitable, and frees 10% of revenue to directly support local veterinary aid, community food networks, and ecological restoration.
