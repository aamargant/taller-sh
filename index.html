Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers to common questions about data privacy, onboarding workload, edge cases, and self-hosting migration.

> How much time does my team need to invest in onboarding?

Under 60 minutes total. We conduct a single technical and operational scoping interview to capture your workflows, edge cases, and access requirements. Our senior engineers handle 100% of the build, tool integration, testing, and deployment.

> How do you guarantee our company data will never leak?

Every deployment is backed by a signed B2B Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and mutual NDA. We exclusively use zero-retention enterprise API endpoints and isolate all persistent knowledge in encrypted European databases (Frankfurt & Paris under GDPR). Your data is never used to train public foundation models.

> What happens when an agent encounters an edge case?

Deterministic safety. Instead of guessing or hallucinating, the agent halts, flags your designated team member via Slack or WhatsApp with full context, and requests guidance. Once guided, the agent saves that resolution as a permanent procedural skill in its database so it never has to ask twice.

> Can we migrate from your managed cloud to our own servers later?

100% portability. Zero vendor lock-in. If you ever choose to bring infrastructure in-house, we provide an export package containing your full Docker compose files, SQLite databases, agent skill definitions, and setup runbooks to run on bare-metal or your private cloud forever.
