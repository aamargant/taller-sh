# GEO and AI Search Standards

1. **AI Crawler Access**: Always keep `robots.txt` permissive for AI search engine bots (`OAI-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `Googlebot`).
2. **LLM Context**: When creating or restructuring core service routes or architecture offerings, update `/llms.txt` and keep it under 200 lines according to the standard.
3. **Structured Data**: Ensure all landing pages have valid JSON-LD schemas describing the Organization, WebSite, and Software/Service blueprints.
4. **Citability**: Structure technical copy with authoritative headings, clear factual bullet points, and verifiable architectural claims that LLM retrieval models can easily parse and cite.
